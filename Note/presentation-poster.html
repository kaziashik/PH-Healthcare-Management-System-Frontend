THE PATH OF ONE VISIT

01

Look

Anyone can search approved doctors by name, specialty, fee, and experience. No account is required.

02

Join

A patient registers and verifies an email code. A doctor applies with a license, password, and resume.

03

Approve

An admin approves or rejects the doctor. Only an approved, verified doctor can sign in and appear in the directory.

04

Open hours

The doctor publishes one date, 3 to 8 hours, with a meeting link. The date locks after publish.

05

Pay

The patient pays the consultation fee with bKash and receives the next free 20-minute serial.

06

Prescribe

After the visit, the doctor records findings and medicines. The patient opens the prescription.

FOUR ROLES ON THE SITE

Patient

The person booking a visit. Signs up on the site.

  • Browses doctors and opens a profile.
  • Books a published schedule on the same day, before it starts.
  • Pays the consultation fee with bKash.
  • Cancels more than 1 hour before the start to get a refund.
  • Opens the prescription after the visit is completed.

Doctor

The physician. Applies on the site, then waits for approval.

  • Applies with a license, password, and resume.
  • Signs in only after the email is verified and an admin approves.
  • Publishes a 3 to 8 hour schedule on one date.
  • Date locks after publish. Time locks after the first booking.
  • Completes the visit and writes findings and medicines.

Admin

Reviews the clinic. Not created from the public sign-up form.

  • Sees clinic counts and the analytics page.
  • Approves a doctor only after that email is verified.
  • Rejects with a written reason. The doctor is emailed.
  • Views the patient list.
  • Cannot create or block another admin.

Super admin

Same clinic desk as an admin, plus control of admin accounts.

  • Creates an admin account.
  • Blocks an admin so that person cannot sign in.
  • Unblocks an admin when access should return.
  • Uses the same doctor review, patients, and analytics pages.
  • Lands on /admin after sign-in, same as an admin.
